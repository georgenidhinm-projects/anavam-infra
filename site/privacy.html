Privacy

Last updated 4 November 2026

This notice covers the anavamgroup.com website and email you send to us. We follow Alberta’s Personal Information Protection Act.

This website

The website has no cookies, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no forms. It doesn’t load anything from other companies.

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. We don’t use that information to identify you. Cloudflare’s own privacy policy explains how it handles this data.

If you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use them only to read and answer your message, and we keep them only as long as we need to. We don’t sell, rent or share them, except where the law requires it. Our email is handled by our mail provider on our behalf.

Your choices

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by writing to hello@anavamgroup.com. If you aren’t satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta.

Changes

If we change this notice, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
